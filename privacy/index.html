Privacy policy

Rugby Team Picker · last updated 9 October 2026

No messaging, by design. Rugby Team Picker has no chat and no private messages. The only notices it gives are about fixtures, cancellations and team sheets. Under-18s join through a parent or carer, and the team admin approves everyone.

This page explains what information Rugby Team Picker (“the app”) holds about the coaches, parents, carers and players who use it, including children, and what you can do about it. It covers the app on iPhone and Android and the web version at cbrear70.github.io/rugby.

Who is responsible

The app is made and run by Chris Brear, who is the data controller for the information the app holds. Clubs and teams that use the app choose their own coaches and admins. Those admins decide who joins their team and what fixtures and team sheets they publish.

Contact: chris_brear@hotmail.com for any question about this policy, or to ask for your information to be seen, corrected or deleted.

What the app holds, and why

The app holds only what it needs to run a team. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

In UK data protection law, the app relies on legitimate interests: running a rugby team’s fixtures and selections, which the people using it expect. Children’s information is used only after a parent or carer has authorised it, and only to let them and their parents see their own fixtures and team sheets.

Children

  • An under-18 can only join a youth team with a one-use invite from a parent or carer who is already linked to them. The team admin then approves their phone.
  • A new adult can only be linked to a child by the team admin, or with the agreement of a parent already linked to that child.
  • A child’s phone can’t leave or unlink itself. A linked parent can remove it at any time.
  • If a child is left with no parent or carer linked, their phone is switched off at once. It sees nothing from the team, and it is deleted after 90 days.
  • There is no way for anyone to send a child a message in the app.

Who can see what

  • Your team: only approved members of a team see its fixtures, squad names and team sheets. A parent sees nothing until linked to their child. A player’s phone sees nothing until the team admin approves it.
  • Parents: a parent sees their own children’s replies and selections, and who else is linked to their children.
  • Team admins: see everyone in their team, their ages and their phones. They also see a log of every change to what players and parents read.
  • Club admins: see their club’s teams and fixtures.
  • Chris Brear, as the app’s maker: can see the stored information to keep the app running and to answer support and privacy requests.

Where it is stored

The information is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), which acts as a processor under Google’s data processing terms. Google may handle the information outside the UK. Google’s terms include the safeguards UK law requires for that, such as standard contractual clauses. The web version is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any website host, receives your browser’s ordinary request details.

On your own phone, the app keeps a copy of your teams in its local storage, so it opens without a signal. Leaving a team removes it from the phone. The app uses no cookies for tracking.

Notifications

In the iPhone and Android app, notifications are worked out on your phone from your team’s fixtures and team sheets: a fixture moved or cancelled, being picked, or replies about to close. Your phone isn’t registered with any outside notification service, and you can turn notifications off in your phone’s settings.

How long it is kept

  • Information stays while you’re a member of a team. A team admin can remove a phone at any time, and phones that haven’t been used for 60 days can be removed in bulk.
  • A switched-off child’s phone is deleted after 90 days.
  • Fixtures, results and team sheets stay with the team, as the team’s history, after a member leaves. A player stays in the squad list, but their phone and its links are removed.
  • Support requests are kept while they are open and for up to a year after, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information the app holds about you or your child, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Email chris_brear@hotmail.com. Requests are answered within one month. Inside the app:

  • a parent can unlink a child, or remove their child’s phone, from the Children tab;
  • leaving a team (Team or Children tab) unlinks a parent and removes their membership;
  • a team admin can remove anyone in their team.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be pointed out in the app.
